Team Fatigue
Each athlete is compared to their own 7-day average, then those percentages are averaged. An athlete joins the calculation only once they have 3 or more jumps in the prior 21 days, so late sign-ups don't distort the line. The grey number on each point is how many athletes it represents — treat a point built on fewer than 5 athletes as noise.
