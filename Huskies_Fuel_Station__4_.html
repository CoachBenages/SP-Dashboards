Defaults: carbs ~3–7 g/kg scaled by training load and goal (ACSM/AND/DC Joint Position Stand, 2016); protein 1.8–2.3 g/kg — 2.0 for muscle gain (intakes above ~2.2 g/kg show no added hypertrophy benefit; Morton 2018), with the fat-loss bump protecting lean mass in a deficit; fat floor of 0.8 g/kg in a deficit for hormonal health. Calories are always derived from the macros (4 kcal/g carb & protein, 9 kcal/g fat), so the dial can never disagree with the gram targets.
